#4471 · two date bugs merged under one subject

owner support desk resolved reviewed 2026-11-01 · 0 days ago

Ticket #4471 was marked as a single known issue because triage deduplication merges incoming reports on subject line alone (decision 0001). Grouping the deduplicated export by tenant locale and booking form channel (#lib/kb/causes and #lib/kb/locales) shows two separate problems inside #4471, tracked in their own tickets.

Two distinct bugs were merged into #4471: parser (tracked in #1) and hint (tracked in #2).

Problems found in #4471

  • parser (ticket #1): The shared date parser reads every date month-first (MM/DD), while every tenant in the directory uses a day-first locale (DD/MM). Dates sent through booking-v2 (app, email, form) or through booking-v3 (app, email) come out with day and month swapped. Reports listed in desk-notes.json (oneDayMove) that read as a one-day move are counted where they are filed under parser by tenant and footnoted below the table.
  • hint (ticket #2): booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, whereas booking-v2 prints DD/MM/YYYY. When customers of day-first tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) book through the form channel, they type the month first as the hint instructs, and the form reads the input the tenant's way (day-first).

Resolution (#4471 · closed 05 oct)

  • parser (ticket #1) — fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales (closed as completed).
  • hint (ticket #2) — won't fix: booking-v3 form retired (closed as not planned).

Why intake tags and subject merging hid the second bug

  • Subject-only deduplication: Under decision 0001, reports with matching normalized subjects merge automatically without reading the body or checking the tenant's booking form.
  • Browser-locale intake guesses: meta.intakeTag is guessed at intake from meta.browserLocale (en-US browsers get tagged hint; European browser locales get tagged parser). The real cause depends on the tenant's locale and booking form plus the report channel (#lib/kb/causes).
